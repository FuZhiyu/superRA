# Slide-Design Planning

Load in PLAN phase when slide work enters the superRA workflow.

## Audience-Context Inventory

Before task decomposition, record the inventory in the governing ancestor task's `## Objective` — review judges the deck against it, and it must reach every descendant:

- **Audience:** venue, likely fields, seniority, and whether they know the paper/project.
- **Shared context:** concepts, methods, data, notation, and institutions the audience knows without setup.
- **Missing context:** concepts, notation, mechanisms, empirical design details, or institutional facts to introduce before they can support a claim.
- **Divergent priors:** phrases or claims likely to read differently across fields.
- **Talk objective:** the one thing the audience should understand or believe by the end.
- **Main-vs-backup policy:** which rigor, robustness, expert objections, and derivations belong off the main path.

The inventory is a planning artifact, not prose for the slides.

## Frontier Contributions

Frontier questions (`superplan §Grilling`): venue, audience makeup, the talk objective, and the main-vs-backup policy.

## Deck Structure

Plan around the audience's context state, not the paper's section order.

- Start with the intended takeaway or question, before details that need that frame.
- Insert context-setting slides ahead of sections where familiar terms can mislead.
- Roadmap returns where the audience needs to reset attention or locate the current section.
- Treat model, data, and empirical-design slides as communication devices: why the audience needs the object comes before its mechanics.

## Task Decomposition

Organize slide-design tasks by communication unit:

- audience/context inventory and deck route;
- title/takeaway pass;
- dense technical-slide simplification;
- overlay and visual-cue pass;
- main/backup split;
- layout/build pass.

Each task names the intended audience state before and after it. "Polish slides" is underspecified without a concrete deck section and communication objective.
