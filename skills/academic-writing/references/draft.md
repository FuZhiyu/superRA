# Draft Mode

1. **Gather inputs.** Use the notes, results, and surrounding text that the section must connect to. Ask for any missing input the claims depend on. Read or inventory the paper's [writing conventions](conventions.md) before drafting.
2. **Outline one level below the headings.** Give each paragraph a purpose before writing it. Use [Structure](structure.md) for section anatomy.
3. **Draft within the author's claims and level of generality.** Use the claims in the supplied material or request. Match the surrounding text's tone, or the author's sample and instructions for a new paper.
4. **Check new prose** against the [Style](style.md) and [Structure](structure.md) checklists. For existing text around it, use the [Draft bar](../SKILL.md#mode-routing) and [Fix tiers](review.md#fix-tiers).
5. **Audit your diff** before completion per [Audit your diff](../SKILL.md#audit-your-diff).
