# Rewrite

Rewriting restructures: the same facts may need a different form, order, or hierarchy. Sentence repair is the smallest of those moves.

Work down the levels — form, order, passage, sentence.

## Re-pick the form

Read what a block carries, not how it is written, then re-pick per [SKILL.md § Choose the form](../SKILL.md#choose-the-form).

- Same fields repeated item after item — a table.
- A paragraph tracking numbers across time, groups, or magnitude — a figure, reading stated beneath it.
- List items each carrying two or three sentences of support — nested bullets.
- Bullets that only parse read in order — a paragraph.

Rewrite the content into the new form. Sentence fragments pasted into cells keep the old defects and add gridlines.

## Re-order the exposition

- **Answer arrives last** — paragraph wall or flat list: rebuild per [SKILL.md § Pyramid structure](../SKILL.md#pyramid-structure).
- **Process chronology** — the outcome leads; the steps that matter sit under it.
- **Template headings** — `Background`, `Approach`, `Results` name slots. Rename each to its claim, then merge the sections that turn out to make one.

## Give every passage one verdict

- **Keep** — unique information the reader needs and this document owns. *The 0.7% match failure, in the results file that produced it.*
- **Point** — content maintained in a linked source. *The merge method, owned by the analysis script.*
- **Merge** — two passages overlap, each carrying a distinct fact. *"252,341 observations" in one bullet, "1994–2023" in another, both about one panel.*
- **Drop** — repetition, outdated state, filler, implementation detail the reader does not need. *"We then re-ran the merge after fixing the path."*
- **Escalate** — incompatible claims or literals, or a deletion that decides an authorial, research, or acceptance question. *Two observation counts for the same table.* Raise it before rewriting; continuing past a conflict picks an answer silently.

Sentence-level defects — indirection, ambiguity, missing actor, a claim outrunning its evidence — get repaired per [SKILL.md § Fix the sentence](../SKILL.md#fix-the-sentence), not dropped.

## Rebuild from the kept facts

1. **Write the answer.** The outcome, decision, or honest current state, no stronger than the source.
2. **Arrange dependencies.** Evidence, caveats, and action sit beneath the claim they support or limit; causal and temporal order survives.
3. **Replace copies with pointers.** Interpretation stays only where this artifact owns it.
4. **Repair the connective tissue.** Only the transitions and relationships the new order requires.
5. **Delete superseded structure.** Stale headings, duplicate summaries, empty sections.

**Make the smallest edit that resolves each defect.** The rule caps the size of each fix, not their number — and a form change is one fix.

## Verify

- **Evidence:** every retained claim the reader may rely on still links its artifact, output, source, or commit.
- **Cold read:** the first layer stands alone; each child has an explicit relationship to its parent.
- **Word reduction alone is not a pass.** Every change makes the answer easier to find or the meaning easier to follow.
