# Friction Audit

Use this audit to remove observable reader cost, not to infer authorship or make prose “sound human.”

## Audit by cost

- **Redundancy:** a unit contributes no new fact, claim, decision, caveat, or action.
  - Merge overlapping units, point to the durable home, or drop the repeat.
- **Indirection:** the reader crosses framing, meta-commentary, process narration, or delayed agency before reaching the claim.
  - State the positive claim and acting subject directly; keep framing only when it changes interpretation.
- **Unsupported abstraction:** importance language, vague attribution, or generalized interpretation outruns the evidence.
  - Name the evidence and bounded claim, or remove the abstraction.
- **Buried structure:** a topic heading, paragraph wall, or flat list hides priority and relationships.
  - Rebuild the visible hierarchy; keep connected reasoning in prose.
- **Ambiguity:** fragments, invented abbreviations, unclear pronouns, compressed dependencies, or inconsistent names force reconstruction.
  - Restore full relationships and one stable term per concept.

## Hold the boundary

- **Name the cost before changing the marker.** Preserve an em dash, technical term, first-person voice, contrast, list, or repeated cadence when it does not slow retrieval or interpretation.
- **Preserve calibrated voice.** Do not flatten domain vocabulary, hedges, emphasis, or authorial rhythm merely because a catalog associates them with AI writing.
- **Reject detector mechanics.** Do not use banned-word lists, punctuation quotas, vocabulary tiers, rhythm targets, type-token targets, personality injection, or repeated passes until markers disappear.
