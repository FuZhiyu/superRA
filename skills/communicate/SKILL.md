# Communicate

**Budget for communication.** Before returning, plan the message as deliberately as the work itself.

## Put the answer first

- **State the answer or current state.** If the work is unresolved, say what is known and what remains open.
  - Nest a caveat directly under the claim it narrows or reverses.
- **Give the support the reader needs.** Include the evidence, limitation, and next action when they change what the reader should conclude or do.
- **Move implementation details to a deeper layer.** Link commands, paths, commits, and methods for readers who need them.

## Keep only useful content

- **Keep a sentence or bullet only when it adds a claim, evidence, caveat, decision, or action.**
  - Preserve words that show cause, contrast, conditions, or sequence.
- **State each fact once.** Put the full detail where it is produced or maintained; link there elsewhere.
- **Support claims the reader may rely on.** Link the artifact, output, source, or commit that supports them.
- **Choose clear over short.** Explain dependencies, surprising results, irreversible actions, and easy-to-miss caveats in full.

## Route the work

- **Choosing the shape:** load [structures.md](references/structures.md).
- **Choosing words and sentences:** load [style.md](references/style.md).
- **Rewriting or distilling existing material:** load [rewrite.md](references/rewrite.md); add [friction-audit.md](references/friction-audit.md) when reviewing why text is hard to read.
- **Source citations, math, tables, or raw HTML:** load [markdown.md](references/markdown.md); add [rich-content.md](references/rich-content.md) for figures.
- **Permanent standalone Markdown:** load [baseline-io.md](references/baseline-io.md). Task-local companions follow `using-superra/references/task-companion-files.md`.
- **Manuscript prose:** compose this skill with `superRA:academic-writing`; that skill owns academic argument, voice, citations, and venue conventions.

## Final pass

- **Read only the opening and top-level bullets.** They should carry the answer or current state.
- **Apply § Keep only useful content once.** Restore any relationship that became unclear.
