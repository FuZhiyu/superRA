# Communicate

## Build the pyramid

- **Decide the reader and action first.** Select only what changes what this reader understands, decides, verifies, or does next.
- **Lead with the outcome, decision, or present state.** Unresolved work leads with what is known and the open branch; never manufacture a conclusion.
- **Put decision-relevant support next.** Give the evidence, material caveat, and next action. Keep a caveat beside the conclusion when it limits or reverses it.
- **Put mechanics last.** Link commands, paths, commits, methods, and optional detail instead of making every reader cross them.

## Select before compressing

- **Keep one durable home per fact.** Put a fact where it is produced or maintained; point to that home elsewhere.
- **Ground material claims.** Link the artifact, output, source, or commit that supports each claim a reader may rely on.
- **Budget structure, not words.** Choose the number of sections, takeaways, or findings the material needs. Cut low-value units; keep complete sentences and exact terms.
- **Choose clarity over brevity.** Expand dependencies, surprising results, irreversible actions, and caveats that compression could hide.

## Route the work

- **Writing or choosing a shape:** load [structures.md](references/structures.md).
- **Rewriting or distilling existing material:** load [rewrite.md](references/rewrite.md); add [friction-audit.md](references/friction-audit.md) when style patterns may be raising reader cost.
- **Source citations, math, tables, or raw HTML:** load [markdown.md](references/markdown.md); add [rich-content.md](references/rich-content.md) for figures.
- **Permanent standalone Markdown:** load [baseline-io.md](references/baseline-io.md). Task-local companions follow `using-superra/references/task-companion-files.md`.
- **Manuscript prose:** compose this skill with `superRA:writing`; that skill owns academic argument, voice, citations, and venue conventions.

## Final pass

**Audit the finished text against §§ Build the pyramid and Select before compressing.** Fix failures before sending or saving.
