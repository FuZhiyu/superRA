## Objective

Skill prose repo-wide matches the terse register of the restyled role skills, the register is taught well enough that agents reproduce it unaided, and every workflow stage's written output respects it.

Register, from the accepted exemplars: bolded imperative + short elaboration; definition bullets over framing sentences; fragments for enumerable actions; no derivation or rationale clauses; cut any clause whose content an adjacent bullet already carries. Target density: implement-task / review-task after their restyles.
